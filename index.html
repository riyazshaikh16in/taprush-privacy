Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

TapRush ("the Game") is a collection of tap mini-games developed by Riyaz Shaikh Labs ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the Game, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The Game has no user accounts and no servers of our own.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Your personal best score for each mini-game is saved locally on your device so you can see your records the next time you play. This data is not sent to us.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, this game data may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account, as controlled by your device settings. We do not have access to that backup.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, which can include:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • Interactions with ads (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's own policies. Ads may be personalized based on this information where permitted and, in the EEA and UK, only according to the consent choice you make (see "Your Choices" below). Learn more about how Google uses data in apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your Choices

  • Consent (EEA and UK): if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time by tapping Privacy settings at the bottom of the Game's home screen.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut or Texas), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising at any time by tapping Privacy settings at the bottom of the Game's home screen.
  • Ad personalization: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Game data: you can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game.

5. No Analytics or Tracking by Us

We do not use our own analytics, tracking pixels, or crash-reporting services. Apart from the Google advertising described above, the Game does not share data with third parties.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load ads.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during gameplay.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help address it.

8. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our systems. Game data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
